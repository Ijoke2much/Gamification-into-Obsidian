DESIGN NOTES

  • Banner — static guild hall (Journey uses road / arena). Same inset frame, different biome.
  • Active contract — one pinned project; reduces ADHD overwhelm.
  • Milestones — 25/50/75/100% gates on existing progress %.
  • Next waypoint — single recommended task (priority / due / energy).
  • Badges — computed: Momentum, Stalled, Urgent, Turn in.
  • CTA — “Continue on Tasks” filters Tasks tab by project: slug.
  • No combat here — Journey/Dungeon keep the fight fantasy.
